# 07: General awareness area

**What to build:** A General awareness 🌍 (green) tile on Home opens 3 levels: L1 fruits and vegetables ("Tap the banana", "Which one is a vegetable?"), L2 animals, their sounds and birds ("Which animal says moo?", "Tap the parrot"), L3 body parts, vehicles and family members ("Tap the nose", "Which one flies in the sky?", "Tap the grandmother"). All visuals are emoji. See the spec: [Bal Vatika Prep](../bal-vatika-prep.md).
